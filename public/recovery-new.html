Start a recovery request

Six steps. Have the asset's IMEI, serial number or plate to hand, plus proof that it belongs to you.

Prove the asset is yours

Enter the details of the VARNIS account the asset belongs to. Any two of the three must match — so a case can still be filed when the owner's phone, SIM or email is gone with the device.

Never share a password you don't own. Two matching fields are enough — leave this blank if it isn't yours to give.

infoWhy it works this way

A stolen phone usually takes the SIM and the email with it. Requiring every field would lock the real owner out at the worst moment; requiring two keeps impersonation hard while leaving a route open for a trusted relative or colleague.
