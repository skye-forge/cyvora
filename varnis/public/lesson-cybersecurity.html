Part 6: Poor Grammar & Professionalism

Analyze why small typos are actually massive red flags for your security.

Look for these errors:

  • warning
    Inconsistent Casing: Mixing "Bank" and "bank" or random capitalization.
  • warning
    Awkward Phrasing: Sentences that feel translated or overly formal in strange places.
  • warning
    Missing Punctuation: Professional emails rarely miss periods or use multiple exclamation points.

Part 7: Hidden Hazards (Attachments)

Attachments are the most common delivery method for malware. Learn how to spot the trap.

dangerous

NEVER open an attachment from an unexpected source, especially compressed (.zip) or executable (.exe) files.

Even common files like PDFs or Word docs can contain macros—hidden scripts that install viruses the moment you click "Enable Editing".

Part 8: Personalized vs Generic

Understand the difference between "Cast-Net" Phishing and Spear Phishing.

Generic (Common)

🎣

"Dear Customer, your account is locked."

Sent to millions. Low success rate, but low effort for the hacker.

Spear Phishing (Targeted)

🎯

"Hi Sarah, here's the report from the 2PM meeting."

Uses your name, job title, or recent activity. Extremely dangerous.

Part 9: Too Good to be True

If it sounds like a miracle, it's probably a threat.

redeem

Inheritance Scam

"A distant relative left a fortune."

work

Job Offer

"$500/hr for remote data entry."

"Legitimate companies do not contact strangers via unsolicited emails to give away large sums of money or high-paying jobs without an interview process."
