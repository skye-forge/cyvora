How it works

Up and running in four steps

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up with a phone number or email, pick French or English, and take a quick 5-question assessment.

  2. 2

    Learn & play

    Work through the zones, take the daily challenge, and earn XP and badges as you go.

  3. 3

    Report what you see

    Spotted a scam? Report it. Every report sharpens the national threat picture for everyone.

  4. 4

    Get certified

    Reach the tier thresholds and download a QR-verifiable certificate to share with your school or employer.
