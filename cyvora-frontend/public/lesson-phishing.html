Module 102: The Anatomy of Phishing

Phishing is the most prevalent form of cyberattack globally. This module breaks down the five core pillars of identification to ensure civic safety and personal data integrity.

Progress will sync with the learning API.

Part 1: Defining the Threat

Phase 1/5

The Visual Definition

Phishing is a fraudulent practice where attackers masquerade as a reputable entity in email or other communication channels to induce individuals to reveal personal information, such as passwords or credit card numbers.

shield

91% of Attacks

All successful cyberattacks start with a phishing email according to national security metrics.

Part 4: Psychological Pressure

Phase 4/5
timer

Time Constraints

Phrases like "Act within 2 hours" or "Immediate action required" bypass critical thinking.

gavel

Threats of Penalty

Claims of account suspension, fines, or legal consequences to induce panic.

warning

Artificial Scarcity

Suggesting a limited window to claim a benefit or prevent a security breach.

The Golden Rule

Legitimate civic and financial organizations will never use threatening language to force an immediate digital action.
